# Customer_Analysis_Dashboard
The main objective of this dashboard is to transform raw customer data into meaningful visual insights that can help businesses understand their customers, improve customer engagement, identify valuable customer segments, and support data-driven business decisions.

The Customer Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and overall customer performance. It provides a clear view of key customer metrics such as total customers, sales, orders, average purchase value, and customer segments.

The dashboard helps identify high-value customers, analyze sales across different customer categories, understand purchasing trends, and compare customer performance by region, gender, age group, or other available attributes.

Interactive filters and slicers allow users to explore customer data based on different dimensions and quickly identify important trends and business insights.

Key Features:
Customer and sales KPI analysis 
Customer segmentation 
Sales and order analysis 
Top customers by revenue 
Customer distribution by region/category 
Purchase behavior analysis 
Monthly or yearly customer trends 
Interactive slicers and filters 
Identification of high-value and low-value customers
